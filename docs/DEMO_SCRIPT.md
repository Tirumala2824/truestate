# Three-minute conversational demonstration

Stay in the same conversation throughout.

**0:00–0:25 — Establish the workspace**
Ask “Give me a simple overview.” Point out the Guided demo label and synthetic data. Explain: matching records can still repeat the same unsupported claim.

**0:25–1:05 — Investigate**
Ask “Investigate Aster’s suspicious payment.” Watch the six roles run actual tools. Expand a tool receipt to inspect its inputs and outputs. The outcome card shows the targeted hold and unaffected payments.

**1:05–1:35 — Understand the evidence**
Ask “Why was it held?”, then “Show the evidence graph.” Ask “Show the signed contract.” The contract appears as an inline source card with its exact evidence ID. Ask “What are the possible explanations?” for uncertainty and competing hypotheses.

**1:35–2:00 — Review the result**
Ask “What should I do next?” and “Give me the audit report.” Download PDF or JSON from the report card. Reviewer releases require a reason and a separate confirmation of one payment.

**2:00–2:30 — Show restraint and failure**
Ask “Try a legitimate bank change.” No fraud hold is needed. Ask “Run the failed containment scenario.” The tools retry once, verify that the hold failed, and escalate honestly.

**2:30–3:00 — Evaluate**
Ask “Run the evaluation,” then “Explain those results simply.” Explain that the 120-case set checks synthetic behavior, not production accuracy. The entire flow has stayed in a single saved conversation.
