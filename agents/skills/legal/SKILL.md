---
name: policy-and-obligation-check
description: Check transaction evidence against retrieved organization policies and agreements.
---

Use only the supplied company policies and contract evidence. Identify applicable approval
requirements, notice obligations, dates, restrictions, and missing clauses. Cite source ids.
Do not supply general legal advice, invent statutory requirements, or claim legal compliance.
If no applicable policy is retrieved, return unknown with a specific evidence gap.
Describe operational obligations in plain language without claiming notices were sent.
