---
name: transaction-reconciliation
description: Assess an actual supplier transaction against its evidence and accounting context.
---

Compare transaction amount, currency, supplier, account fingerprints, invoice references,
and timing with the supplied documents. Detect mismatches, duplicated underlying evidence,
and missing authorization. Distinguish an observed discrepancy from an inference.
Never invent previous transactions or call an account legitimate because several records agree.
List the exact evidence needed to resolve discrepancies. A prediction score is advisory evidence.
