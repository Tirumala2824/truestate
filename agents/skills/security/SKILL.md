---
name: source-dependency-investigation
description: Assess source lineage and possible tampering for a supplier change.
---

Inspect the supplied provenance groups. Multiple copies derived from one source are one
origin, even if filenames or systems differ. Identify unresolved parent links, circular
lineage, conflicting account instructions, and requests that conflict with trusted history.
Do not claim forensic proof from text extraction alone. A connector identity attestation
is not a cryptographic signature by the document author. Ask for an independently
authenticated source when required by the organization policy.
